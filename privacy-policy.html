Gemstellar Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 7, 2026

Gemstellar ("the game") is made by Emvani Group LLC ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information is collected when you play, who collects it, and the choices you have. We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, or phone number through the game.

Age

The first time you open the game, you choose one of three age ranges: under 13, 13–15, or 16 or older. The buttons are equal. Nothing is pre-selected. The choice is saved on this device and is not sent to us. Settings › Reset progress does not clear it. Deleting the game does.

  • Under 13: the game does not start Google AdMob and does not ask for tracking permission. Ads stay off.
  • 13–15: ad requests are tagged for users under the age of consent. We do not show Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt.
  • 16 or older: in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and US states where Google's User Messaging Platform message applies, the game asks before it requests ads. On iPhone and iPad, Apple's tracking prompt is shown when ads are allowed and you have not answered it yet.

Outside those regions, players who are 16 or older can see personalized ads without that message. Players 13 or older can still reset the Android advertising ID, or change iPhone tracking, in system settings.

Information stored on your device

Your progress (coins, Diamonds, scores, trophies, streaks, and settings) and your age-range choice are saved on your device. We do not receive them. Settings › Reset progress erases progress. If Android backup is turned on, Android can include that on-device data in your own Google account backup. We do not receive the backup.

This version of the game does not use Firebase Analytics or Crashlytics.

Your choices

  • Ad privacy (EEA, UK, and US states where Google shows a message): you are asked when ads are set up. Change it anytime in Settings › Privacy settings. That row is shown when Google says a privacy-options entry is required. It is not shown when ads are off.
  • iPhone and iPad, ages 16 and older: you choose whether to allow tracking. Change it anytime in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking. Ages 13–15 are not shown that prompt.
  • Android: reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings › Privacy › Ads.
  • Remove Ads: a one-time purchase turns off interstitial and banner ads. Rewarded videos stay available, because you choose to watch them.

Children

Gemstellar is not directed at children under 13. The age question is how we keep advertising and tracking off for that group. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 through ads. Players aged 13–15 are treated as under the age of consent for European ad settings. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help remove it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or California law), you may have the right to access, correct, or delete personal information, or to opt out of its sale or sharing for targeted advertising. Where Google's message applies, Settings › Privacy settings is the control for ad choices. You can also email us and we will help you reach each provider.

Security and retention

Data sent by the game travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Providers keep data according to their own retention policies. Progress and the age-range choice remain on the device until you erase them, and may be included in your Android backup as described above.

Changes

We will post any update to this policy on this page and change the effective date above.
