Gemstellar Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 16, 2026

Gemstellar ("the game") is made by Qskonnect LLC ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information is collected when you play, who collects it, and the choices you have. We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email address or phone number through the game.

Information stored on your device

Your progress (coins, Diamonds, scores, trophies, streaks and settings) is saved only on your device. We do not receive it. Deleting the game or using Settings › Reset progress erases it.

Information collected by our service providers

ProviderWhat it collectsWhy
Google AdMobDevice advertising identifier (only if you allow it), IP address, approximate location derived from IP, device and app information, ad interactionsTo show ads, measure them and prevent fraud. Ads are personalized only where you have given permission.
Google Firebase (Analytics and Crashlytics)App interactions (for example rounds played and levels reached), an app instance ID, device and OS information, crash reportsTo understand how the game is played and fix crashes
RevenueCatAn anonymous app user ID and your purchase receiptsTo confirm purchases and restore them on a new device
Apple App Store / Google PlayPayment details for purchasesTo process payments. We never see your payment card.

Each provider handles data under its own policy: Google, RevenueCat, Apple.

Your choices

  • Ad personalization (EEA, UK and some US states): you are asked for consent the first time you play. Change it anytime in Settings › Privacy choices.
  • iPhone and iPad: you choose whether to allow tracking when the game first opens. Change it anytime in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.
  • Android: reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings › Privacy › Ads.
  • Remove Ads: a one-time purchase turns off interstitial and banner ads.

Children

Gemstellar is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help remove it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or California law), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal information, or to opt out of its sale or sharing for targeted advertising. Because we do not hold your data ourselves, the fastest route is the choices above. You can also email us and we will help you reach each provider.

Security and retention

Data sent by the game travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Providers keep data according to their own retention policies.

Changes

We will post any update to this policy on this page and change the effective date above.
