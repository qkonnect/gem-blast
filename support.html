Gemstellar Help and Support

Email us at qskonnect@gmail.com. We reply within two business days. Include your phone model and what happened.

I bought Remove Ads but I still see ads

Open Settings (⚙️) › Restore purchases. Make sure you're signed in to the same Apple ID or Google account you bought it with.

I got a new phone or reinstalled the game

Remove Ads comes back with Restore purchases. Coins, Diamonds, scores and trophies are saved only on the phone they were earned on, so they don't move to a new device.

I was charged but didn't get my Diamonds

Close and reopen the game first. If they're still missing, email us with the date and the receipt from Apple or Google.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by the store: Apple or Google Play.

How do I change my ad privacy choices?

In the game: Settings › Privacy choices (shown where required). On iPhone: Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking. See our privacy policy.

What are the odds in mystery crates?

50% +25 coins · 25% +60 coins · 13% +1 Meteor · 9% +3 Diamonds · 3% +15 Diamonds. Crates are earned in play and cannot be bought.

How do I delete my Gemstellar data?

Gemstellar has no accounts. Your progress (coins, Diamonds, scores, trophies, streaks and settings) is stored only on your phone. To delete it, use Settings › Reset progress in the game, or uninstall Gemstellar.

To ask us to delete data held by our service providers (Google AdMob, Google Firebase and RevenueCat), email qskonnect@gmail.com with the subject "Gemstellar data deletion" and the name of the device you play on. We will process the request within 30 days. Analytics and crash data is deleted; purchase records are kept by Apple, Google and RevenueCat as long as required for tax and legal reasons. You can also reset or delete your Android advertising ID in Android Settings › Privacy › Ads.
